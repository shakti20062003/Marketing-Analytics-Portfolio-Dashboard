# Marketing Analytics Portfolio Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This project presents an **end-to-end Marketing Analytics dashboard** built using **SQL, Python, and Power BI** to analyze digital marketing performance for a simulated **e-commerce business** selling sports and fitness products.

The dashboard provides actionable insights into:
- Conversion performance
- Social media engagement
- Customer reviews and product ratings

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
To help marketing and product teams:
- Understand how users move through the engagement funnel
- Identify high and low performing products
- Track monthly trends in conversions and engagement
- Analyze customer satisfaction through reviews and ratings

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Tracked

### 🔹 Conversion Metrics
- Overall Conversion Rate: **8.5%**
- Monthly conversion rate trend
- Conversion rate by product

### 🔹 Social Media Performance
- Total Views: **~2.98M**
- Total Clicks: **~458K**
- Total Likes: **~73K**
- Monthly trends for Views, Clicks, and Likes

### 🔹 Customer Reviews
- Average Rating: **3.7**
- Rating trends by month
- Product-wise average ratings

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Conversion Analysis
- Seasonal fluctuations observed in monthly conversion rates
- Certain products (e.g., Kayak, Ski Boots) show significantly higher conversion performance
- Identifies products with strong engagement but weaker conversion potential

### Social Media Engagement
- Views peak during early and mid-year months
- Click-through and likes show gradual decline toward year-end
- Highlights drop-off between views and clicks for funnel optimization

### Customer Review Insights
- Ratings remain relatively stable across months
- Product-wise ratings reveal customer satisfaction differences
- Helps identify products needing quality or positioning improvement
